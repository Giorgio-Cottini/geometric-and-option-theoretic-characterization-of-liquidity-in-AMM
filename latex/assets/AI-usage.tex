Artificial intelligence tools were used as auxiliary instruments during the
preparation of this thesis. In particular, they supported code development and
debugging, graph production, the discovery and contextualization of relevant
literature, LaTeX formatting, and linguistic and coherence checks.

They were not used to define the research question, guide the research process,
interpret empirical results, evaluate the assumptions or conclusions of prior
studies, or generate the final thesis text. All final content, including the
analysis, interpretation, conclusions, and written exposition, remains the sole
responsibility of the author.

The tools used were Consensus for literature discovery and source
contextualization, ChatGPT for explanations, linguistic revision, and
LaTeX-related assistance, and Claude for code-related assistance, including
debugging, refactoring, and optimization.
