# Jim Gatheral

Professor of financial mathematics at Baruch College (CUNY), known for foundational work on implied-volatility surfaces and rough volatility.

## Role
- Presidential Professor in the mathematics department at Baruch College (CUNY) and a leading figure in volatility modeling.
- Author of the arbitrage-free SVI parametrization and a co-originator of the rough-volatility paradigm.
- Supplies the volatility-surface machinery the thesis reuses when it recasts AMM liquidity provision as an options position with an implied-volatility structure.
