# Tai-Ho Wang

Mathematician working on the theory of automated market makers, jointly affiliated with Baruch College (CUNY) and Ritsumeikan University. He is the central recurring author across the thesis's CFMM-pricing sources.

## Role
- Faculty in the mathematics / financial mathematics area at Baruch College (CUNY), with an appointment tied to Ritsumeikan University.
- The connective author of the thesis's core theoretical line: he develops the mathematical framework that treats liquidity provision in constant-function market makers as an options-and-reserves problem, and grounds it empirically on Uniswap v3 data.
- His work supplies the reserve/option duality and intrinsic-liquidity lens that the thesis builds on.
