# Olivier Gueant

Olivier Gueant is a researcher working on market making, optimal execution, and automated
market maker design, with a body of work spanning classical dealer market making and the
extension of that theory to decentralized exchanges. His work develops the stochastic-control
and Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman tools that underlie Stackelberg-style equilibrium models of
liquidity provision, connecting the classical market-making literature to the automated market
maker setting this region studies.
