# a16z Crypto

The crypto-focused venture capital and research arm of Andreessen Horowitz, which funded and hosts part of the loss-versus-rebalancing (LVR) research program.

## Role
- Runs a research group (led by Tim Roughgarden) that produces academic work on AMM design and decentralized-finance mechanisms.
- The institutional backer behind key LVR papers the thesis relies on, connecting the LP-cost analysis to an industry research agenda.
