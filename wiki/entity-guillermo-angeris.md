# Guillermo Angeris

Guillermo Angeris is a researcher on the convex-analytic theory of constant function market
makers, co-author of [[source-replicating-market-makers]] and [[source-geometry-of-cfmms]]. His
work establishes the Fenchel-conjugacy and conic-duality correspondence between LP payoff
functions and CFMM trading functions, the theoretical basis this thesis direction uses to treat
bonding curve shape as a design variable.
