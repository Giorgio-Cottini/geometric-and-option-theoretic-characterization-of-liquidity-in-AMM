# Jason Milionis

Computer science researcher at Columbia University and the lead author of the loss-versus-rebalancing (LVR) line of work on automated market makers.

## Role
- PhD researcher in the Columbia CS theory / algorithmic-economics group.
- Lead author on the papers that introduce and quantify loss-versus-rebalancing, the adverse-selection cost that liquidity providers bear against arbitrageurs.
- His LVR framework is the thesis's canonical account of the LP cost that CFMM pricing theory must offset with fees.
