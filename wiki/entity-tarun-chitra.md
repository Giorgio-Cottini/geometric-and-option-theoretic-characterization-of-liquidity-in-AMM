# Tarun Chitra

Tarun Chitra is a researcher on CFMM theory, co-author of [[source-replicating-market-makers]]
and [[source-geometry-of-cfmms]] alongside [[entity-guillermo-angeris]]. He works at Gauntlet, a
firm focused on blockchain protocol risk and parameter design, and his papers with Angeris supply
the convex-duality and geometric-axiom foundations that let a CFMM's bonding curve be recovered
from, or matched to, a target liquidity provider payoff.
