# Jimmy Risk

Researcher in quantitative finance and a co-author of Tai-Ho Wang and Shen-Ning Tung on the CFMM pricing/hedging and Uniswap v3 liquidity-surface work.

## Role
- Contributes to the pricing and hedging theory of liquidity provision in constant-function market makers and to its empirical validation on Uniswap v3 data.
- Part of the author group that formalizes LP payoffs through the reserve/option-duality lens central to the thesis.
