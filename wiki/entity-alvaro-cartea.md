# Alvaro Cartea

Academic researcher in mathematical finance and algorithmic trading, and the lead author of the paper that derives predictable loss and the closed-form optimal liquidity range for concentrated-liquidity providers. His work applies stochastic control to market-making and execution problems, and the paper cited here extends that program to decentralized automated market makers.
