# Ciamac C. Moallemi

Professor at Columbia Business School and a principal co-author of the loss-versus-rebalancing (LVR) research on automated market makers.

## Role
- Faculty in the Decision, Risk, and Operations division at Columbia Business School, working on market microstructure and quantitative finance.
- Co-author on the core LVR papers that formalize and quantify the adverse-selection cost of AMM liquidity provision.
- Brings a market-microstructure framing to the thesis's account of LP economics.
